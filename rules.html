20 Rules of School

  • Arrive at school on time every day.
  • Wear the proper school uniform.
  • Respect your teachers and staff.
  • Be kind and respectful to your classmates.
  • Listen carefully when someone is speaking.
  • Raise your hand before speaking in class.
  • Complete and submit homework on time.
  • Bring all required books and supplies.
  • Keep your classroom and school clean.
  • Do not litter; use the dustbins.
  • Walk, don't run, in the corridors.
  • Do not bully, tease, or fight with others.
  • Take care of school property.
  • Do not cheat in exams or copy others' work.
  • Keep mobile phones switched off during class.
  • Stay in class unless you have permission to leave.
  • Be honest and tell the truth.
  • Follow safety instructions at all times.
  • Respect everyone's belongings; never take what isn't yours.
  • Take part in class activities and always do your best.
