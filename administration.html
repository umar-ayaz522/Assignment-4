School Administration Policy

  • Admissions are open to all eligible students according to the published criteria.
  • Parents or guardians must submit complete and accurate admission documents.
  • School timings must be followed by students, teachers, and staff.
  • Minimum 75% attendance is required to appear in final examinations.
  • Absence must be reported with a written application or a call from a guardian.
  • All fees must be paid before the due date each month or term.
  • A late fee fine may be charged on delayed fee payments.
  • Fee concessions are given only on merit or genuine need, after approval.
  • The school uniform and ID card are compulsory for all students.
  • Visitors must register at the front desk and get permission before entering.
  • Parents can meet teachers only during the scheduled meeting hours.
  • Teachers must prepare lesson plans and complete the syllabus on time.
  • Staff must maintain accurate records of attendance, marks, and results.
  • Examinations are conducted fairly, and any cheating leads to disciplinary action.
  • Report cards are shared with parents at the end of each term.
  • Bullying, harassment, and discrimination are strictly prohibited.
  • Complaints can be submitted to the administration and are reviewed promptly.
  • Students leave the school only with a guardian or with written permission.
  • Fire safety, first aid, and emergency procedures are practiced regularly.
  • The administration may update this policy and will inform parents and staff of changes.
